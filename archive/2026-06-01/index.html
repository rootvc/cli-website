From the Founding Charter

Root Ventures is constituted to support the earliest stages of those engineers who, demonstrating uncommon technical nerve, accept the risk inherent to attempting things not yet known to work.

« Seeding bold engineers. »

Stated more plainly: a San Francisco–based deep tech seed fund. As engineers ourselves, we specialise in leading initial funding for founders tackling new technical opportunities. Sector concentrations include deep tech, hard tech, robotics, manufacturing, automation, AI/ML, and devtools.

Acquisitions methodology & conservator's notes

The museum maintains a strict acquisitions policy: a candidate specimen must demonstrate genuine engineering risk — that is, the founders must be attempting something whose feasibility is not yet established. The Curators conduct technical inspection in the founders' workspace where possible; in the case of remote or hazardous habitats (low earth orbit, the open ocean, energy infrastructure perimeters) the conservators rely on field reports and direct correspondence.

Specimens are accessioned at the earliest reproducible sighting and are followed thereafter at intervals not less than quarterly. Reserves are held against subsequent observed growth; the museum's reserves ratio is two thirds of fund capital, allowing meaningful re-acquisition of viable specimens through later instars. The cadence of new acquisitions is deliberately slow — a selective few per year — in order to ensure each specimen receives appropriate curatorial attention.

Visitors who wish to nominate a specimen for consideration are directed to the Registrar's Office (see Inquiries, below).

Vacancy: Venture Capital Associate

The museum seeks one technical Associate to join the curatorial staff in San Francisco. The position involves sourcing of candidate specimens, technical diligence, and direct support of accessioned companies.

Suitable applicants will possess:

  • A strong technical background (CS degree, engineering experience, or equivalent).
  • Genuine curiosity about emerging technologies and startups.
  • Excellent communication — the ability to explain complex technical matter simply.
  • Hustle and resourcefulness. You figure things out.
  • A passion for working alongside technical founders.

Additional consideration is given to those who have built side projects, contributed to open source, or operated within prior startups. Inquiries may be addressed to the Registrar's Office; see below.
